## 一、大作文三大载体全景总览与卷别分布

考研英语大作文（Section B）区别于小作文书信体系的核心在于**“试题信息的载体形态”**。考纲历史上共出现三大试题载体：

| 载体类型 (Carrier) | 卷别主阵地 | 历年考察年份 | 核心命题特征与考查重点 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. 图画作文 (drawing)** | **英语一绝对主流** | 英一：2006~2023, 2024（图画+图表组合） | 寓意漫画、单图或双图对比、文字标识/对话。考查“微观画面观察 ➔ 抽象社会现象/哲理引申”。 |
| **2. 图表作文 (chart)** | **英语二绝对主流** | 英二：2010~2025 全覆盖；英一：2024（组合题）、2025（数据表格） | 柱状图、饼状图、折线图、数据表格。考查“数据极值提取 ➔ 结构反差与趋势透视 ➔ 宏观经济社会动因归纳”。 |
| **3. 文字材料作文 (material)** | **命题前沿新风向** | 2025 英一大纲新题型方向 | 短文材料、寓言故事、社会议题导语。考查“主旨提炼 ➔ 辩证立意 ➔ 逻辑论证与价值判断”。 |

## 二、载体一：图画作文（Drawing / Cartoon）解构与写作公式

图画作文是考研英语一长盛不衰的经典载体。其本质是**“看图提炼深层隐喻”**，绝不能停留在画面表层的物理动作描述。

### 1. 首段：客观描述 + 象征引申黄金公式（约 35~45 词）
- **第 1 句（全景定位与主体动作）**：
  - `In the center of the cartoon stands [Subject] who is [doing Action], while [Contrast / Background Scene].`
  - *例*：`In the center of the cartoon stands a young boy performing Peking Opera with intense passion, while beneath the platform sit merely two elderly spectators, leaving the remaining vast hall completely vacant.`
- **第 2 句（图画文字/台词点睛，如有）**：
  - `The caption / dialogue underneath the drawing vividly points out: "[Caption/Dialogue]".`
- **第 3 句（象征寓意引申绝杀句）**：
  - `Evidently, this subtle visual narrative takes on a profound overtone of [Theme/Issue] amid the contemporary epoch.`
  - *例*：`Evidently, this subtle visual narrative takes on a profound overtone of the cultural dilemma confronting intangible heritage amid the digital epoch.`

### 2. 次段：多维深度归因论证公式（约 100~120 词）
- **承上启下首句**：`A myriad of interrelated factors contribute to this thought-provoking phenomenon, among which [Primary Factor] plays a pivotal role.`
- **动因 1（主观心理/内在动机）**：`In the first place, from an individual perspective, [Mental Mechanism] often prompts people to [Behavior]...`
- **动因 2（客观环境/时代科技推力）**：`In the second place, on a broader societal level, the rapid proliferation of [External Environment] has profoundly reshaped [Lifestyle/Mindset]...`
- **正反论证/后果延展**：`Consequently, without timely and proactive intervention, [Negative Consequence] risks escalating into [Severe Crisis].`

### 3. 末段：建设性两手抓对策与展望公式（约 35~45 词）
- **结论引导句**：`To reverse this disquieting trend / To maximize the benefits of this positive momentum, concerted endeavors ought to be mobilized across society.`
- **对策 1（个人觉醒/行业创新）**：`On the one hand, individuals / practitioners should [Specific Action] to [Positive Goal].`
- **对策 2（体制支持/社会引导）**：`On the other hand, governmental authorities and educational institutions are expected to [Policy/Education Measure].`
- **倒装收尾绝杀句**：`Only through such two-pronged synergy can [Ultimate Vision] be successfully sustained for generations to come.`

## 三、载体二：图表作文（Chart / Data Matrix）解构与写作公式

图表作文是考研英语二的基石题型。其本质是**“用学术英文翻译数据关系”**，严禁枯燥地把每个百分比念一遍，必须提炼**“极值（最大/最小）”与“结构反差（升降/剪刀差）”**。

### 1. 四大图表形态与数据提取黄金法则
1. **柱状图（Bar Chart）**：重在“横向柱状对比”与“纵向跨年变动”。必须抓取：峰值项（Top 1）、谷值项（Bottom 1）、增幅最大项与降幅最大项。
2. **折线图（Line Chart）**：重在“发展走势与转折拐点”。必须抓取：起止点净增量、平稳起步期 vs 加速井喷期、转折分水岭年份。
3. **饼状图（Pie Chart）**：重在“各部分占比与结构主导性”。必须抓取：占据绝对统治地位的板块（>50% 或前两项合并）、占比极微的板块。
4. **数据表格（Table / Data Matrix）**：重在“代际反差或群体剧烈分化”。必须抓取：两极极端反差（如中年员工满意度 0% vs 不满意度 64%）。

### 2. 首段：图表主题 + 核心极值数据与反差公式（约 35~45 词）
- **第 1 句（图表类型与宏观主题引申）**：
  - `The bar chart / pie chart / table clearly illuminates the significant changes in [Topic] between [Year] and [Year].`
- **第 2 句（核心极值与主导趋势汇报）**：
  - `As is explicitly demonstrated by the statistics, [Category A] experienced a phenomenal surge from [Data 1] to [Data 2], successfully claiming the lion's share of [Total].`
- **第 3 句（结构反差与对比特征）**：
  - `In sharp contrast, [Category B] witnessed a dramatic plunge from [Data 3] to [Data 4], highlighting a profound structural transformation in [Domain].`

### 3. 次段：宏观社会经济动因论证公式（约 100~120 词）
- **引领句**：`The statistics depicted above mirror a pervasive socioeconomic trend in contemporary China, which can be attributed to several driving forces.`
- **动因 1（居民收入与消费观念升级）**：`First and foremost, China's sustained economic growth and the continuous rise in disposable personal income have enabled consumers to shift their focus from mere survival necessities to high-quality experiences.`
- **动因 2（产业政策赋能与基础设施完善）**：`Furthermore, targeted governmental policies and modern technological infrastructure have considerably lowered market barriers, thereby stimulating unprecedented growth in [Industry].`

### 4. 末段：前瞻趋势预判与理性倡议公式（约 35~45 词）
- **趋势预判**：`Taking into account the aforementioned analysis, it is predictable that this upward trajectory will maintain its strong momentum in the foreseeable future.`
- **理性引导**：`Nevertheless, both market regulators and consumers ought to maintain rational vigilance, ensuring that such rapid expansion proceeds in a healthy, inclusive, and sustainable manner.`

## 四、载体三：文字材料作文（Material / Text Prompt）解构与写作公式

2025 年全国统考英语一大纲样题开辟了“文字材料”新方向。没有图画，也没有图表，而是给出一段 50~100 词的文字材料（如社会热点评论、寓言小故事、哲学对谈），考查考生的纯语言思辨与立意概括能力。

### 1. 首段：材料主旨概括 + 核心命题破题公式（约 35~45 词）
- **第 1 句（引述材料主干）**：
  - `As is vividly delineated in the written narrative, [Summarize Story/Dialogue in 1 sentence].`
- **第 2 句（提炼材料深层矛盾或核心观点）**：
  - `The central thesis reflected by this passage underscores a timeless philosophical dilemma: [Dilemma / Key Stance].`
- **第 3 句（立论切入）**：
  - `This subtle illustration prompts us to re-examine our perceptions regarding [Core Topic] in modern society.`

### 2. 次段：辩证剖析与立论支撑公式（约 100~120 词）
- **正面立论（内在价值）**：`To delve deeper, the significance of [Core Virtue/Action] cannot be overemphasized. In our rapidly evolving world, [Reason 1]...`
- **反面论证（忽视之后果）**：`Conversely, when people surrender to [Superficial Temptation / Cynicism], they are prone to [Negative Impact]...`
- **现实映射**：`A telling case in point is [Brief Example / Contemporary Trend], which eloquently testifies to this truth.`

### 3. 末段：个人立场重申与行动指引（约 35~45 词）
- **总结重申**：`From what has been expounded above, the conclusion becomes self-evident.`
- **行动倡议**：`We should consciously cultivate [Mindset], balancing [Aspect A] with [Aspect B], and translating high aspirations into steady daily actions.`

## 五、载体别名自动归一化字典（Alias Reference）

系统在 CLI 与后台交互中支持多样化载体名称输入，底层自动归一化为三大法定载体枚举：

| 法定枚举 (Canonical) | 支持的用户与私教输入别名 (Aliases) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`drawing`** | `cartoon`, `picture`, `photo`, `image`, `draw`, `图画`, `漫画`, `图片` |
| **`chart`** | `graph`, `table`, `bar`, `pie`, `line`, `data`, `matrix`, `图表`, `柱状图`, `饼图`, `折线图`, `表格` |
| **`material`** | `text`, `passage`, `reading`, `quote`, `narrative`, `文字`, `材料`, `材料作文` |
